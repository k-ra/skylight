# Skylight — principles

What we believe, in the order we learned it. Read this before changing anything.

**A project drawn as a sky.**
Galaxy → star system (an architecture department) → constellation (a feature). A star is a thing to do, a question, or an exploration. Ships are agents. The orange star pinned at the bottom is HQ, the orchestrator. Those are the only words.

**Position is meaning.**
Closer in is more done. Because position says something, nothing is draggable. The picture and the truth cannot disagree.

**Activity is not progress.**
Commits are motion. Only two things cannot be inflated: a test that holds, and a person who said *live*. Nothing counts commits, tests, or tokens as achievement.

**Nothing is drawn that is not true.**
No placeholder examples, no illustrative states. An empty sky is an honest sky.

**Colour means alive. Motion means someone is working.**
The screen stays quiet. Orange is the orchestrator and nothing else. Hover freezes motion.

**One file a person writes. Everything else derives.**
`sky.yaml` and the repository. Rings come from files and tests, ships from transcripts or presence reports, explorations from git. Hand-typed status is the first thing to go stale, so there is none.

**A person writes `live`. Nobody else.**
The orchestrator writes `seen`, `context`, `near`, and to-dos marked `by: orchestrator`. It never deletes a line a person wrote, never answers its own question, never marks anything done.

**A dotted line is a promise. A solid line is implementation.**

**Say it where you are.**
Double-click the sky to place a star. Kind is implied by where you put it. No forms, no drop-down menus in the corner.

**Copy is short.**
Labels are two or three words; the long line is for hover. Nobody reads a sentence on a star. Counts are marks, not sentences.

**Finishing means no longer needing detail.**
Zoom out and a finished constellation is one bright star. Finished things are not named, ranked, or scrubbed through time.

**Your space is a folder.**
It runs on your machine. Nothing in the UI ever shows an absolute path from someone's disk. Sharing is a deliberate act with a key that dies when you stop.

**Fun is allowed, and it must be true.**
Dawn comes when nothing waits on you. Meteors carry real news. Sparks fly when something actually lands.
